# Code of Conduct

This project follows a simple standard: be respectful, stay on topic, and do not harass anyone.

## Expected

- Clear, constructive feedback
- No personal attacks, spam, or off-topic advertising
- No attempts to extract secrets, abuse APIs, or steal this product

## Enforcement

Maintainers may hide comments, close issues/PRs, or block accounts that violate this standard or [LICENSE](LICENSE).

Security reports belong in private channels described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not in public issues.
